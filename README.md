## 🌱 a little about me


I started with a B.Tech in CS at SRM, where I learned to code and somehow ended up falling into ML.

Now I'm finishing my M.S. in Computer Science at **Arizona State University** 🌵, where I've been spending most of my time around ML systems, AI, and cloud infrastructure.

Lately I've been getting a little too curious about open source — contributing, experimenting, and occasionally breaking things just to see what happens.

I like taking things out of the notebook and seeing what happens when they meet the real world.

And if something doesn't make sense to me, there's a pretty good chance I'll disappear into a rabbit hole until it does.

<br>

👉 **Curious what I've built? Take a look through my repos — there's a little bit of ML, AI, backend, cloud, and random experiments hiding in there.**
